The Problem

Project managers are flying blind

Modern tech teams generate enormous amounts of project data — boards, tasks, sprints, OKRs — but turning that data into actionable PM insight requires hours of manual work every week.

🔍

No Single View

Project status is scattered across Monday boards, Slack threads, sprint notes, and OKR docs. No PM dashboard shows the full picture.

🐢

Late Risk Discovery

Blockers, overloaded team members, and at-risk OKRs are often discovered too late — after the sprint is already failing.

🔗

No Strategy Connection

Daily tasks are disconnected from OKRs. The team is busy but it is unclear whether the work actually moves business goals forward.

The Solution

Monday Smart PMO Agent

An AI-Powered PMO Assistant for Project Visibility, Risk Monitoring and Strategic Alignment. Ingests Monday-style project data, analyzes it through a PMO engine, and gives project managers and PMO stakeholders the visibility they need.

Human-in-the-Loop

The agent assists. You decide.

The Monday Smart PMO Agent supports PMO decision-making but does not replace human judgment. It detects risks, summarizes information, highlights gaps, and suggests actions — but a human PM or PMO lead must approve decisions, escalations, and delivery changes.

✅

What the agent does

Detects risk signals · Summarizes sprint status · Highlights OKR gaps · Surfaces team overload · Generates executive summaries · Suggests follow-up actions

🚫

What the agent does NOT do

Make delivery decisions · Approve or reject work · Automatically escalate issues · Replace the PM or PMO role · Reassign tasks autonomously

👤

Human approval required for

Escalations to leadership · Sprint reprioritization · Resource reallocation · Delivery timeline changes · Any action based on agent output

PM Methodology

Grounded in real PM practice

The Monday Smart PMO Agent is built around recognized project management methodologies. The AI knowledge base includes structured documentation on each area.

🔄

Agile & Scrum

Sprint ceremonies, roles, backlog management, Definition of Done, and how a PMO supports Agile teams without disrupting self-organization.

📅

Sprint Planning

Capacity calculation, dependency mapping, Sprint Goal definition, scope commitment vs. stretch goals, and mid-sprint re-scoping criteria.

🔁

Retrospectives

Retro formats (Start/Stop/Continue, 4Ls, Sailboat), action item tracking, anti-patterns, and how to measure retro effectiveness over time.

🎯

OKR Framework

Writing good objectives and key results, OKR grading, cascading from company to team, aligning sprint work to strategic goals, and avoiding common OKR mistakes.

👥

Capacity Management

Focus factor calculation, workload distribution, bus factor analysis, overload detection, and how capacity planning connects to sprint health and risk.

📊

PMO Visibility

How a PMO provides value in Agile environments — metrics, dashboards, escalation paths, portfolio-level OKR tracking, and executive reporting.
